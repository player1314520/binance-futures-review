# Open 2.0 constitution

1. Use explicit capability flags; never infer evidence quality from an ordinal level.
2. Unobservable data is neither passing nor absent. Show the limitation and deny unsupported conclusions.
3. Synthetic market data cannot support MFE/MAE or claims about real market behavior.
4. Market differences belong in declared descriptors, not hidden UI branches.
5. Do not aggregate money across currencies without an explicit, evidenced conversion contract.
6. Preserve shared-engine output compatibility or make a reviewed versioned change with regression evidence.
7. Prefer an empty value with a concrete reason over fabricated zeroes, NaN, or endless calculation states.
8. Do not add exchange rebates, referral incentives, or volume-linked monetization.
9. Payment, promotion, and operating-market decisions require separate legal and compliance review.
10. External processing must be declared before implementation and remain consistent with the public
    [privacy and network promise](../PROMISE.md).

## Honest boundaries

1. These rules guide the Open candidate; they do not technically enforce every future contribution.
2. Passing local tests does not prove data completeness, live-account correctness, or regulatory compliance.
3. Any changed promise, network path, or monetization model requires a new review and cannot rely on this text alone.
